✳A slightly opinionated directory

Useful tools.
No magic tricks.

A field guide to apps that can help you learn, research, make, code, and create presentation slides. Each entry includes a practical idea and a link to its official home.

YOUR MILEAGE MAY VARY. YOUR TABS WILL NOT.

10 tools on the list. Your mileage may vary.

◎General assistant

ChatGPT

A flexible assistant for questions, explanations, drafts, brainstorming, coding, and working with files or images.

ΓAI presentations

Gamma

Turn a prompt, outline, or notes into a designed presentation, then edit it and export it for PowerPoint.

The fine print (tiny but sincere)

Useful assistant.
Not an oracle.

These are suggested ways to try each tool, not promises that every feature is available on every plan or in every country. Software enjoys character development.

Check each official site for current features, pricing, and privacy controls. Treat generated answers as a starting point, especially when the stakes are higher than choosing a font.
